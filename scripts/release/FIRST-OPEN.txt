Opening Magic Cut for the first time

1. Open MagicCut.dmg and drag Magic Cut onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Magic Cut and choose Open, then Open again.

macOS asks once because Magic Cut is not from the App Store and carries no Apple developer certificate.

If there is no Open button:
  open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Magic Cut was blocked",
  click Open Anyway, and confirm.

After that it opens like any app, and it updates itself when a new version is out.
