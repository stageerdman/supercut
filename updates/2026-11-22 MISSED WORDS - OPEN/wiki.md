# Missed words — wiki

Durable lessons for this update. Status lives in `update v1.md`.

## Principles (from the design)
- **Silence means safe to cut; unheard speech means something was said.** They must never look the same, in any view.
- **Derive, don't store.** Unheard speech and pauses come from speech runs and words; neither is edit state.
- **What's highlighted is what's replaced.** The same `wordsIn` middle rule decides highlighting and replacing.
- **Text never moves time it didn't change.** Unchanged words keep their times; new words take free time, preferably
  the sound in it.
